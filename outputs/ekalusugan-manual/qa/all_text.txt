EKalusugan User Manual  
Patient Dentist Staff and Administrator instructions  
Version 1.0   |   18 October 2026 
Use this manual to sign in, understand your dashboard, and complete the tasks assigned to your role. It 
covers the current dental queue, walk-in registration, patient records, clinical documentation, reports, 
account management, and SMS notifications. 
 
Figure 1  EKalusugan homepage 
1  Log in opens the sign in page.   2  Why E Kalusugan jumps to the feature overview.   3  Your next visit jumps to the sign in 
invitation. 
Read the access instructions first, then follow the chapter for your role. Red numbered labels identify the 
controls explained below each screenshot. Names, contact details, and account identities in the 
screenshots are anonymized. Example queue and clinical records illustrate actions in the actual running 
interface. 
The patient portal currently handles same-day dental queue tickets and appointment history. Clinic 
administrators provide accounts; public self-registration and future-date patient booking are not available in 
this version.

Using this manual 
Before you start, obtain the EKalusugan website address and an active account from the clinic. Use a 
browser with an internet connection. Keep your registered mobile phone available if the clinic sends your 
first temporary password by SMS. 
A button name in these instructions matches the system text. A disabled button appears faded and cannot 
be used until its requirements are met. Read any message near the button before retrying. Close or Cancel 
in a dialog generally returns to the previous screen; Continue or a save button confirms the described 
action. 
Choose your role 
Role Main responsibilities Begin with 
Patient Request and follow your own dental ticket, review appointment history, and find 
the clinic. Chapter 2 
Dentist or Doctor Set availability and daily limits, manage the dental queue, save consultation 
notes, enter clinical counts, and export reports. Chapter 4 
Staff Find patients, register dental walk-ins, manage arrival and queue actions, 
review records, and verify in-person recovery. Chapter 3 
Administrator Oversee queues and activity, manage accounts, view history and forecasts, and 
configure SMS alerts. Chapter 5 
 
Read the screen correctly 
The system uses Doctor as the account role and Dentist for the dental service. A ticket number identifies a 
queue visit. An appointment record and a clinical report are separate records. A blank, dash, or loading 
indicator does not confirm that a count is zero. 
Dates and queue return times use Philippine time. Patient and Staff screens refresh about every 15 
seconds; Dentist screens refresh about every 30 seconds. Read the latest state before acting because 
another authorized user may have updated the same ticket. 
Protect patient information 
Use your own account. Verify the selected patient before opening or changing records. Keep passwords 
private, share a recovery password only with the verified patient, and use Log Out when finished. Keep the 
public queue monitor on the dedicated display and keep account and clinical screens at the staff 
workstation.

Set or change your password 
 
Figure 5  Set or change your password 
1  Current Password enter the password you currently use.   2  New Password use at least eight characters including a 
number.   3  Confirm New Password enter the same new password again.   4  Update Password saves the new password. 
1. At first activation or recovery login, the system opens Change Your Password. In this setup flow, the form

Note  A temporary-password setup session is time limited. If it expires, return to reception or request the 
appropriate activation assistance.

password while signed in.

Understand the patient dashboard 
 
Figure 7  Patient dashboard 
1  Get queue ticket opens the dental service selection.   2  Appointment history displays earlier appointments.   3  My queue 
number shows your active ticket.   4  Doctor availability shows the current availability status.   5  Location opens the clinic

After signing in with a patient account, E-KALUSUGAN opens the patient portal. The home screen brings 
together today's dental queue, your own ticket, dentist availability, and appointment history. 
1. Read the large Now serving and Next in queue cards. When a patient has been called and is awaiting 
arrival confirmation, the first card reads Called  -  report to staff. 
2. Check My queue number for your active ticket number and selected dental sub-services. A dash means 
there is no active ticket to show. 
3. Read Doctor availability. The card may show Checking availability, Available today, or Unavailable today. 
Availability alone does not guarantee that registration or daily slots remain open. 
4. Use Get queue ticket to join today's dental queue when the button is enabled. Use Appointment history to 
review your visits. 
5. On a narrow phone screen, use the Toggle menu button at the top to show the navigation controls. Tap 
your name to reveal Log Out when you finish using the portal. 
Note  The queue and dentist availability refresh automatically every 15 seconds while the portal remains 
open. 
Note  The Estimated wait card displays ~15 min. Treat this as an indicative display; keep watching the 
queue and respond when called. 
Note  An existing waiting, called, serving, or missed ticket counts as an active queue. You cannot request 
another active ticket. 
Note  Help, Notification, and CHO services are visible on the current home screen. There is no additional 
help, notification list, or services action to complete from those controls in this version.

Request a dental queue ticket 
 
Figure 8  Request a dental queue ticket 
1  Dental services choose one or two services.   2  Selection counter shows the two service limit.   3  Get Queue Ticket 
submits the request.   4  Close returns to the dashboard. 
Use this process for a dental visit on the current day. Choose the services you need, then let the system 
assign the ticket number. 
1. From the patient home screen, select Get queue ticket. 
2. In Get Queue Ticket, read any registration message shown above the service list. 
3. Select one or two checkboxes under Dental services. The counter shows how many services you have 
selected. Once two are checked, the other checkboxes are disabled; uncheck one before choosing a 
replacement. 
4. Select Get Queue Ticket at the bottom. The button reads Getting your ticket... while the request is being 
processed. 
5. When the request succeeds, the window closes and your ticket appears on the dashboard. Check the 
queue number, your name, selected services, and current status. 
6. Keep the portal open or check it again while waiting. Follow the displayed queue and report to staff when 
your status says It's your turn! 
Note  At least one service is required. No more than two services can be selected. 
Note  Close queue ticket (the X icon), the Escape key, or clicking outside the dialog closes it without issuing 
a ticket. 
Note  A valid priority category on your patient record is applied automatically. Ask clinic staff if your 
recorded priority details need review. 
Note  The system refuses a new request when you already have an active queue today. Patient self-service 
is limited to two queue registrations per day, including earlier registrations that ended. 
Note  Registration can be refused if there is no dentist, the dentist is unavailable, or today's appointment 
slots are full. 
Note  After today's dental queue has started, new same-day appointment registration closes. The displayed 
message directs patients to approach staff for walk-in registration. A patient who already has an active 
appointment for today may still be eligible to request a ticket.

• Found care elsewhere 
• Transportation or access issue

Note  Continue remains disabled until a reason is selected.

Find an existing patient 
 
Figure 14  Find an existing patient 
1  Find an existing patient search before creating a new record.   2  Full name enter or confirm the patient name.   3  
Confirmed mobile number verify the number with the patient. 
Always check whether the person already has a record. Reusing the correct record keeps the person's visits 
together and helps prevent duplicates. 
1. In Register dental walk-in, enter the patient's name or mobile digits in Find an existing patient. A name 
search needs at least two characters; mobile searches can use any four or more digits. 
2. Wait for Searching patient records… to finish. The form can also look for matches as you enter Full 
name, Date of birth, or Confirmed mobile number. 
3. Compare each Possible existing patients result with the person in front of you. Results show a name, 
birthday, and masked mobile number. 
4. Select the correct result. The form shows Returning patient, fills the patient details, and lists the number 
of Previous dental visits with up to three recent entries. 
5. Review and confirm the patient's current details and mobile number before submitting. If you selected the 
wrong record, select Clear selection and search again. 
6. Use Open patient profile when you need to see the identity details and full queue history before 
proceeding. 
7. If the results are different people, check I checked these records; create a new patient. only after 
checking them. Then complete the new patient's details. 
Note  The form will stop registration if likely matches exist and neither a patient nor the explicit new-record 
confirmation has been selected. 
Note  Changes made to a selected patient's name, birthday, gender, mobile number, barangay, or priority 
category are saved with walk-in registration. Check these fields carefully; they are not just temporary ticket 
labels. 
Note  The selected patient's queue must not already be active today.

Register a dental walk in 
 
Figure 15  Register a dental walk in 
1  Dental services select one or two required services.   2  Priority use only for a verified eligible patient.   3  Register and 
assign dental queue creates the visit and issues a number. 
This process assigns a same-day dental queue ticket to an existing or newly recorded walk-in patient. 
1. Find and select the existing patient first, or confirm that a new record is needed. 
2. Enter or confirm Full name, Date of birth, Gender, Barangay, Bago City, and Confirmed mobile number. 
The full name must include first and last names. Birthday cannot be in the future. 
3. For Confirmed mobile number, enter the ten digits beginning with 9 in the field beside +63. Confirm the 
number directly with the patient before registration. 
4. Under Dental services, select one or two services. Scroll within the service list when necessary to see all 
choices. 
5. Under Queue type, choose Regular or Priority. When selecting Priority, select a Priority category: Senior 
citizen (60+), PWD (Person with Disability), or Pregnant. 
6. Select Register and assign dental queue. While the request is processing, the button reads Registering 
patient… and the form is disabled. 
7. Read the green registration message. Tell the patient the assigned queue number and initial position, 
and relay the exact SMS instruction shown. 
8. Check that the new ticket appears in the dental queue. The form clears after a successful registration so 
it is ready for the next patient. 
Note  The form requires all patient-detail fields, a valid ten-digit mobile entry beginning with 9, one or two 
services, and a category when Priority is chosen. 
Note  The service can refuse registration when the dentist is unavailable, walk-in slots are full, a record is 
archived, or that patient already has an active queue today. Read the displayed error, correct the issue, and 
retry only when appropriate. 
Note  The success message may say no queue SMS will be sent. In that case, tell the patient to stay 
nearby. Otherwise explain that one SMS will be sent as the turn approaches and that serving order may 
change. 
Note  Registration creates or reuses a patient record and assigns a queue ticket. A new walk-in does not 
automatically receive a portal login account.

Note  The queue list may change when another authorized user acts. If an error appears, read it and 
refresh; confirm the current state before taking another action.

Handle missed calls and returning patients 
The interface enforces two calls and waiting intervals before a missing patient is skipped. A first missed turn 
gives the patient a short return period. 
1. After the first call, check Called patient controls. It shows Call 1 of 2 and a Wait countdown. 
2. If the patient has not arrived, wait at least 30 seconds until Call again is enabled. Select Call again to 
make the second call. 
3. After the second call, wait another 30 seconds. If the patient is still absent, select Skip and call next when 
enabled. 
4. The absent patient's first missed turn moves to Missed patients  |  10-minute return window. The next 
waiting patient is called as part of the skip action. 
5. If the patient reports back before the listed Report before time, select Patient returned for that exact 
ticket. The ticket re-enters Waiting and will be called at the next opening. 
6. If the window expires, Patient returned becomes disabled and the ticket ends as a no-show. If a patient 
returns but misses the next called turn as well, the ticket also ends as a no-show. 
Note  Use Patient present only for someone physically here. Use Patient returned only after the missed 
patient has reported back. 
Note  Each call is announced twice; Call again is the separate second call controlled by the 30-second wait. 
Note  The return period begins when the ticket is skipped. Continue serving the queue while the missed 
patient has time to return. 
Note  A missed ticket remains an active ticket during its return period, so the patient cannot hold another 
active ticket at the same time. 
Cancel a ticket with a reason 
1. Find the correct ticket in Up next. Confirm the queue number and patient name. 
2. Select Cancel at the right of that ticket. 
3. In Reason for cancellation, select a reason from the list, then select Continue. 
4. Review the refreshed queue. If the cancellation was not intended, select Cancel in the reason window 
before submitting. 
Note  A reason is required; Continue is disabled until one is selected. 
Note  The staff dashboard's visible Cancel action appears on waiting rows in Up next. It is not shown as a 
separate button on the current-serving or missed-patient cards. 
Note  Cancellation reasons are the same choices listed in the patient cancellation section. They are 
recorded with the visit and may appear in queue history.

Assist a verified patient with recovery 
Restore access after checking the patient's identity in person. 
1. In Accounts > Patients, select Account recovery under the verified patient's name. 
2. Read Patient profile and compare the record with the patient. Check name, date of birth, gender, 
registered mobile number, barangay, and city. 
3. Select Start account recovery. 
4. Ask the physically present patient to provide their details. Confirm all six checks only when they match: 
patient present, first name, last name, registered login phone, address, and birthday. 
5. Select Issue temporary password and review the confirmation that the old password and all existing 
sessions will be invalidated. 
6. Hand the displayed temporary password directly to the verified patient. The result also shows the 
registered login number and expiry in Philippine time. 
7. Explain that the temporary password expires in 30 minutes and works for one login. After signing in, the 
patient must choose a new password within 15 minutes. 
8. Select Hide temporary password when handover is complete. Closing hides it permanently. Use  Admin 
dashboard to return. 
Note  Recovery requires an active account, a complete identity record, a registered account phone, and a 
record that has not been archived into another patient. 
Note  If the patient details differ or identity is uncertain, stop the recovery flow. 
Note  Issue temporary password is disabled until every verification checkbox is checked. 
Note  The result disappears at expiry. Hand it directly to the verified patient and keep it private. 
Note  The profile also shows Queue history. Duplicate-record merging appears only for eligible unlinked, 
unarchived records; it moves every queue visit and archives the source record. Verify the destination 
carefully before confirming any merge. 
Follow the illustrated reception checklist in Chapter 3 for the verification screen. Activation SMS 
replacement and in-person account recovery have different actions; select the workflow that matches the 
patient's account state.
