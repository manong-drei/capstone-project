CHAPTER I 
INTRODUCTION 
Background of the Study 
Healthcare service management involves various processes that support the delivery 
of services to patients, including appointment scheduling, patient queuing, provider 
availability, and the management of healthcare records. These processes help healthcare 
facilities organize patient visits and coordinate available healthcare resources. Appointment 
scheduling research has examined the challenges of coordinating patient demand with 
healthcare provider availability, while queuing studies have explored approach es for 
managing patient flow and waiting time (Gupta & Denton, 2020; Ata & Chen, 2022). The 
use of historical healthcare data has also created opportunities for predictive analytics, 
which can provide information about future patient demand and support hea lthcare 
planning (Chen et al., 2021; Wang, 2023). These areas provide the foundation for the 
development of digital solutions that can support more organized healthcare service 
delivery. 
Current healthcare practices combine traditional procedures with technology-based 
approaches. Online appointment systems provide an alternative to manual appointment 
scheduling, while digital queuing systems can assist in organizing and monitoring patient 
flow. Digital systems have also been developed to support provider availability and 
schedule management. In the Philippines, related studies have explored smart queuing for 
local healthcare services, automated patient flow monitoring, digital queuing, prov ider 
schedule management, and real -time staff availability monitoring (Bacolod City Health 
Office, 2023; Davao Medical Center, 2022; Quezon City Health Department, 2024; 
Batangas State University, 2024; Cebu Institute of Technology, 2023). Related systems 
have also demonstrated the use of web -based appointment scheduling and healthcare 
management platforms, while predictive analytics has been applied to patient demand, 
registration volume, and patient influx (Tufail, 2020; Perpetual Help Medical Center, 2021; 
Chen et al., 2021; Agnihotri & Taylor, 2021; UP Manila Health Informatics, 2023). These

developments show that digital technologies are being applied to different aspects of 
healthcare service management. 
Despite these developments, the reviewed literature and related studies indicate a 
problem fit concerning the integration and localization of these functions within a specific 
healthcare setting. Existing studies and systems address appointment scheduling,  queuing, 
provider availability, predictive analytics, and SMS -based notifications through different 
approaches, but they do not specifically address the combined workflow of dental services 
at the Bago City Primary Care Facility. In addition, the reviewed predictive analytics studies 
focus on patient demand, registration volume, patient influx, and no-show behavior, while 
the proposed study focuses on predicting annual dental patient volume according to age 
group using historical dental data and the ARIMA model. Based on the proponents' 
observation of the existing processes at the Bago City Primary Care Facility, manual 
appointment and queue procedures may also make it difficult to coordinate scheduled and 
walk-in patients, monitor queue status, and determine dentist availability. These conditions 
establish a contextual gap in applying an integrated digital approach specifically to the 
dental service workflow of the facility. 
To address these identified needs, the proponents propose E -KALUSUGAN: A 
Web-Based Appointment Scheduling System for Bago City Primary Care Facility with 
Predictive Analytics. The proposed system will provide online and on -site dental 
appointment scheduling, structured queuing for scheduled and walk -in patients, real-time 
queue monitoring, dentist availability management, and centralized patient and 
appointment records. It will use historical dental data and the ARIMA model to predict 
annual patient volume according to age group, providing information that may assist the 
dental department in planning its services. The system will also include a prepared SMS 
notification component for future integration with an SMS service provider, together with 
a mobile-responsive interface and essential online and offline capabilities. Through these 
functions, E -KALUSUGAN is designed as a localized digital solution for the specific 
dental service workflow of the Bago City Primary Care Facility.

Objectives of the Study       
The main objective of this study is to design and develop E -Kalusugan, a web 
application appointment scheduling and queuing system for the Bago City Primary Care 
Facility, aimed at improving patient flow, enhancing scheduling efficiency, and supporting 
healthcare service delivery.  
The following are the specific objectives of the study;       
1. To develop a web-application appointment scheduling system that allows patients 
to schedule dental consultations online and enables staff to manage dentist 
availability and scheduled appointments. 
2. To implement a structured, queuing system that accommodates both scheduled and 
walk-in patients, and provides queue status monitoring. 
3. To integrate predictive analytics using the ARIMA model and historical dental data 
to predict annual patient volume according to age group, providing information that 
may assist in dental service planning.

Scope and Delimitation      
This study focuses on the development and implementation of E-Kalusugan, a web-
based system designed for appointment scheduling and queuing at the Bago City Primary 
Care Facility, specifically for its dental services. While the system is engineered with a 
scalable architecture intended for facility -wide use across all medical  departments, this 
research centers on the dental department as its primary implementation environment. The 
scope includes patients residing within Bago City seeking dental care and the healthcare 
staff responsible for managing these consultations. The system is developed using React for 
the frontend, Node.js and Express.js for the backend, and MySQL for database 
management. Its features include an online appointment booking module, a real-time doctor 
availability tracker, and a structured queuing system th at accommodates both walk-in and 
scheduled patients. Its primary purpose is to enhance efficiency, accessibility, and service 
delivery within the facility.

Delimitations 
The study is limited to appointment scheduling and queuing for dental services at 
the Bago City Primary Care Facility. It does not cover other healthcare services offered by 
the facility and does not replace actual dental consultations, examinations, diagn osis, or 
treatment. 
The SMS notification component is limited to its prepared functionality and does 
not currently provide actual SMS delivery until an SMS service provider is integrated. The 
system does not integrate with other healthcare facilities, national health systems,  external 
hardware devices, third-party applications, or standalone mobile applications. 
Users are limited to accessing their own appointment details and queue status to 
support privacy. The accuracy of real -time dentist availability depends on timely updates 
provided by authorized health center staff or the dentist. The predictive analytics component 
is limited to predicting patient volume by age group  only using the historical dental data 
available for the study, and its accuracy depends on the quality and completeness of the 
available data. 
Access to the system requires a smartphone, tablet, or computer for its web -based 
functions. Development and testing of the software are limited to the academic timeframe

Significance of the Study 
  The study addresses the need for a more organized and accessible approach to 
managing dental services at the Bago City Primary Care Facility. By providing a web-based 
platform for appointment scheduling, queuing, patient record management, dentist 
availability monitoring, and patient volume, the proposed system may help improve the way 
dental services are managed and accessed. The study is expected to provide benefits to the 
following: 
  For Patients of BCPCF. The system may provide patients with a more convenient 
way to schedule dental appointments and monitor their queue status. It may help reduce 
unnecessary waiting by allowing patients to view their appointment and queue information 
through the system. The SMS notification component may also provide appointment and 
queue updates once SMS service integration is implemented. 
  For Dental Staff  and BCPCF Staff . The system may assist dental staff in 
organizing appointments, managing both scheduled and walk-in patients, monitoring dentist 
availability, and maintaining patient and appointment records. These features may help 
reduce reliance on manual procedures and  support more organized daily dental service 
operations. 
  Bago City Primary Care Facility.  The system may provide the facility with a 
centralized platform for managing dental appointments, queues, and related patient records. 
Its predictive analytics component may also predict patient volume according to age group, 
which may help the dental department anticipate service demand and support resource 
planning. 
  Future Researchers and Developers. The study may serve as a reference for future 
researchers and developers who plan to develop similar systems involving appointment 
scheduling, queuing, and predictive analytics in healthcare settings. The system may also
