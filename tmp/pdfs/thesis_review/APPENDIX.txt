APPENDIX H 
ADVISER’S ACCEPTANCE FORM 
   This manual guides you through signing in and navigating the E-KALUSUGAN dashboard. 
It explains how to manage dental appointments, monitor the patient queue, register walk-in patients, 
access patient records, document dental consultations, generate reports, manage user accounts, and  
Log in 
           Log in Screen                                                     Change Password Screen 
1. Open the clinic website and select Log In. Use the account provided by the clinic. 
2. Enter your registered Phone Number and Password, then select Log In. The system 
automatically opens the dashboard assigned to your account role. 
3. If prompted to set a new password, enter and confirm a password containing at least eight 
characters and one number. Select Update Password to save it. 
Password Setup: During initial activation or recovery, the form asks for New Password and 
Confirm New Password. The regular password-change form also requires your Current 
Password, as shown in the screenshot.

No Account or Login Error: Contact clinic reception for assistance. Public self-registration is

When Finished: Open your name or profile menu and select Log Out. Keep your password 
private and do not share it with other users. 
Forgot Password 
                 Recovery Instructions                                           Reception Identity Checklist 
For the Patient 
1. Select Forgot Password? on the login screen to view the recovery instructions, then visit 
the clinic reception. 
2. Verify your identity with authorized staff by providing your name, registered phone 
number, address, and birthday. 
3. Receive the temporary password privately from authorized staff. Return to the login 
screen and sign in using your registered phone number and the temporary password 
within 30 minutes. The temporary password is valid for one login only. 
4. After signing in, create and confirm a new password within 15 minutes. Select Update 
Password to save your new password and complete the process.

For Authorized Staff and Administrators 
1. Open Account Recovery. Staff can select Account Recovery, search for the patient, and 
open the matching profile. Administrators can navigate to Accounts > Patients > 
Account Recovery. 
2. Start Account Recovery. Select Start Account Recovery and verify the patient's 
physical presence and identity details: first name, last name, login phone number, address, 
and birthday. 
3. Confirm the identity details. Check all six verification items only when the information 
matches the patient's records and the required verification has been completed. 
4. Issue the temporary password. Select Issue Temporary Password and review the 
confirmation before proceeding. 
5. Hand over the password securely. Provide the temporary password directly to the 
verified patient. Once the patient has received it, select Hide Temporary Password to 
conceal it on the screen. 
Before Issuing a Temporary Password: The patient must have an active and eligible account. 
Stop the process if any identity details do not match. Issuing a recovery password replaces the 
previous password and signs out existing sessions. 
Important: Selecting Forgot Password? only displays the in-person recovery instructions; it 
does not send a password reset link. For login problems involving Doctor, Staff, or Admin 
accounts, contact the system administrator.

Patient 
Patient Dashboard 
1. Check Doctor availability, then select Get Queue Ticket when enabled. 
 
2. Choose one or two dental services, then select Get Queue Ticket. 
3. Check the assigned number, selected services, and status on your dashboard. 
Note: If registration is closed or full Read the message and approach staff for assistance. You 
can hold only one active ticket. Patient self-service allows up to two queue registrations per day, 
subject to availability. The patient portal handles today's dental queue. Future-date patient 
booking is unavailable.

Ticket 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
1. While Waiting, stay nearby, watch the queue, and follow the SMS instruction on your 
ticket. Queue order and estimated waiting time may change. 
2.  When It's your turn! appears, report to staff immediately. Receiving care means your 
arrival has been confirmed. 
 
3. After a Missed call, report to staff within the displayed 10-minute return window. Staff 
must select Patient returned; refreshing your portal does not confirm a return. 
4. To cancel a Waiting or Missed call ticket, select Cancel Queue, choose a reason, and 
select Continue. For a called or serving ticket, speak to staff. 
Note: Other patient tools Select Appointment history to review your visits. Select Location, 
then Open in Google Maps, to find the clinic. Follow the on-screen queue even when an SMS is 
expected.

Doctor 
Use the Doctor account for the dental queue and consultation notes. 
 
1. In Currently Serving, select Next when ready. Check the called ticket and patient name. 
2. When the patient is physically present, select Start consultation. Use Consult to reopen 
notes for a serving visit. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
3. Enter the optional  Consultation Notes, then the Diagnosis and Prescription / 
Recommendations as appropriate. 
4. Review every field and select Save Consultation. A successful save completes the 
serving visit. 
Note: Absent patient Wait 30 seconds, select Call again, wait another 30 seconds, then use Skip 
and call next if still absent. Use Patient returned only after the patient returns before the 
displayed deadline.

Doctor Settings and Report 
1. On the dashboard, select the current Today's Availability status to switch Available or 
Unavailable. Check the saved state. 
 
2. Set Appointment Limit from 0 to 10 and Walk-In Capacity from 0 to 30. Select Apply and 
wait for Saved. Recheck the limits each day. 
 
 
3. Open Analytics and choose Today, This week, or This month and the required date. 
4. For clinical totals, open  Daily barangay entries, choose the date and barangay, enter 
verified counts, and select Save entries. 
5. Review both report pages, then select Print report or Download Excel once data is ready. 
Use Appointment History to review Today or All appointments.
