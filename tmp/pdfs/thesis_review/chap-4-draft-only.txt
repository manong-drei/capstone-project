CHAPTER IV 
RESULTS AND DISCUSSIONS 
The proponents of the developed system conducted a comprehensive system 
evaluation to test the functionality and features of E-Kalusugan based on expert insights.

beneficiary of the developed application. Using a Likert scaling user questionnaire, the 
proponents gathered valuable feedback from IT experts, healthcare administrators, dental 
staff, and patients regarding system performance in terms of Usefulness, Satisfaction, and 
Ease of Use.

System Testing 
System testing was conducted using a user questionnaire that employed a Likert 
quantitative approach to assess the system's usefulness, based on how the system was 
developed and its alignment with documentation standards. Experts and users provide 
feedback to the system to address current manual issues and enhance automation 
processing based on target user needs. Provide satisfaction to the target user by ensuring

intuitive interactions, and a friendly approach.

Implementation Plan 
  The proponents scheduled formal coordination meetings with administrators at the 
Bago City Primary Care Facility to plan the deployment of E -Kalusugan. E-Kalusugan 
comprises a web-based administrative dashboard for healthcare personnel and a mobile -
responsive portal for patients across all 24 barangays of Bago City. Implementation 
required configuring workstation computers at the facility, ensuring stable internet 
connectivity, setting up the SMS gateway API, and initializing database master records. 
Facility staff were provided with user manuals and operational guides during formal 
training sessions.

Implementation Results 
 The implementation of E -Kalusugan significantly enhanced appointment 
scheduling, queue management, and patient record tracking at the Bago City Primary Care 
Facility. Clinic staff reported notable reductions in patient overcrowding, as real-time SMS 
alerts and digital queue numbers allowed patients from all 24 barangays to arrive closer to 
their actual consultation times. Furthermore, the integrated predictive analytics module 
provided facility administrators with clear forecasts of patient volume categorized by age 
group and dental servic e requirements, aiding in optimal staff allocation and resource 
planning
